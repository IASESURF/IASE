# International outreach kit

Goal: grow the IASE network of instructors and partner surf shops outside the US.

## 1. Who to invite first

Start with surf destinations that already have large surf-school industries and many international visitors:

| Region | Priority destinations |
|---|---|
| Europe | Portugal (Ericeira, Peniche), France (Hossegor, Biarritz), Spain (Canary Islands, Basque Country) |
| Asia-Pacific | Indonesia (Bali, Lombok), Japan (Chiba, Miyazaki), Australia, Philippines (Siargao), Sri Lanka, China (Hainan) |
| Americas | Costa Rica, Mexico, Brazil, Peru, Nicaragua |
| Africa | Morocco, South Africa |

Where to find contacts: surf-school listings on Google Maps for each town, Instagram location tags, booking sites for surf camps, and recommendations from current members (the referral form on `join.html`).

## 2. A simple weekly routine

1. Pick one destination per week. List 15–20 surf schools and shops there (name, email or Instagram, website).
2. Send the invitation below in the local language, with one personal line about their school.
3. Follow up once, after 7 days. Don't send more than one follow-up.
4. Log every contact in a spreadsheet (date sent, reply, status).
5. When a shop joins, ask it to introduce two or three other schools nearby.

Only email businesses about relevant partnerships, honor any request to stop, and follow local rules on marketing email (for example GDPR in Europe).

## 3. Offer something concrete

People join faster when the benefit is clear. Consider:

- A free directory listing for the first year
- Recognition of existing certifications from other systems at an equivalent IASE level, instead of starting from Level 1
- Hosting IASE courses as a paid exam center
- A founding-partner badge for the first shop in each country

>
> We'd love to invite [School name] to join. Partners get a free listing on our global map of certified instructors and shops, can become IASE schools and certify students, and are recommended to students who are looking for a certified surf school.

>

Track monthly: invitations sent, replies, applications received, members listed, countries represented, and course bookings by country.
